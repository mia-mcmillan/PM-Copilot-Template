# 2025-Q4 Product Roadmap: Tempo Engage

**Period**: Q4 2025 through Q2 2026
**Last Updated**: November 20, 2025
**Status**: Approved by Leadership Team

---

## Strategic Context

### Strategic Themes (Prioritized)

**1. Social Fitness** (Primary Focus)
- **Hypothesis**: Social accountability drives sustained wellness engagement in remote teams
- **Evidence**: Team participants have 2.8x better retention than individual users
- **Focus**: Team-based features, async social mechanics, peer accountability

**2. Democratize Corporate Wellness** (Secondary Focus)
- **Hypothesis**: Inclusive design (all fitness levels, no equipment) unlocks broader market
- **Evidence**: Generic programs fail (18% open rate), customers reject equipment-based solutions
- **Focus**: Accessibility, flexibility, affordability

**3. AI Personalization** (Future Focus)
- **Hypothesis**: Personalized coaching increases engagement for diverse fitness levels
- **Evidence**: "One-size-fits-all" cited as pain point in 3/5 customer interviews
- **Focus**: Adaptive difficulty, personalized recommendations, constraint-aware programming

### Market Dynamics

**Key Pattern**: [[Engagement Drop-off - Pattern]]
- 73% sign up → 18% active after 60 days (82% churn rate)
- Root cause: Lack of social accountability in remote environments
- Competitors (Wellable, Gympass) have same problem (18-31% MAU)

**Competitive Timing**:
- Q1 2026: Wellable contracts expiring, HR leaders making renewal decisions
- Compelling event: CFOs demanding wellness ROI or cutting budgets
- Window: October-December to capture evaluation cycles

## Now / Next / Later Roadmap

### NOW (Q4 2025): Team Challenges MVP

**Priority**: P0 (Highest)
**Timeline**: October 1 - December 15, 2025 (10 weeks)
**Theme**: Social Fitness

**Core Opportunity**: [[OPP-101 - Team Fitness Challenges]]

**Scope**:
- Mobile-first team challenge platform (iOS + Android)
- Pre-built challenge templates (7-day, 14-day, 30-day formats)
- Team competition mechanics (leaderboards, team rankings, progress tracking)
- Async social features (team chat, reactions, milestone celebrations)
- Wearable integration (Apple Watch, Fitbit, manual entry fallback)
- Basic admin dashboard (participation metrics by team/department)

**Success Metrics**:
- **Engagement**: 60%+ MAU (vs. 18-31% for competitors)
- **Retention**: 70%+ users active after 90 days
- **Pilots**: 20 paid pilots @ $10K each (3-month commitment)

**Dependencies**:
- Wearable API access (Apple HealthKit, Fitbit API)
- HRIS integration partners (Workday, BambooHR)
- Mobile app store approvals (2-week lead time)

**Target Launch**: December 15, 2025

### LATER (Q2 2026 & Beyond): AI Personalization & Expansion

- **Customer Growth**: 50 total customers, $2.5M ARR (end of Q2)

**Dependencies**:

- Content production (video shoots, editing, instructors)

**Target Milestones**:
- Apr 15: AI coach beta (50 users)

## Roadmap Rationale & Trade-offs

### Why Team Challenges NOW?

**✅ Reasons to Prioritize**:
1. **Critical pain validated**: 4/5 HR Directors facing budget cuts, need solution by Q1 2026
2. **Compelling event**: Wellable contracts expiring Dec 31, decisions made Nov-Dec
3. **Competitive weakness**: Incumbents have 18-31% MAU, we have proof of 2.8x better retention
4. **Clear ROI**: Team challenges directly solve engagement drop-off pattern
5. **Platform foundation**: Social features enable future personalization (can't personalize if nobody engages)

**❌ Trade-offs Made**:
- **Delayed AI personalization**: Could solve "one-size-fits-all" pain earlier
  - *Rationale*: Social accountability is bigger driver of retention (2.8x vs. personalization unknown)
- **No content library in MVP**: Could differentiate with workout videos
  - *Rationale*: Challenge mechanics > content (focus on engagement, not library size)
- **Limited wearable support**: Only Apple Watch + Fitbit in MVP
  - *Rationale*: 80% market coverage, manual entry fallback, can add others later

### Why ROI Dashboard NEXT?

**✅ Reasons to Sequence Here**:
1. **Pilot feedback**: Learn from Q4 pilots before building advanced features
2. **Sales enablement**: Case studies + ROI data needed to scale beyond 20 pilots
3. **Customer retention**: HR leaders need to justify spend to CFOs in Q1 budget cycles
4. **Competitive moat**: Better ROI measurement = stickier customers (harder to switch)

**❌ Trade-offs Made**:
- **Delayed international expansion**: Could capture UK/EU earlier
  - *Rationale*: Validate product-market fit in US first, then expand
- **No enterprise features**: Could sell to larger companies (1000+ employees)
  - *Rationale*: SMB market (200-800 employees) is easier to penetrate, faster sales cycles

### Why AI Personalization LATER?

**✅ Reasons to Delay**:
1. **Data dependency**: Need 6+ months of workout data to train ML models
2. **Engagement prerequisite**: Personalization only helps if people stick around (solve retention first)
3. **Resource intensity**: AI requires ML engineers, data scientists (hire in Q1 2026)
4. **Validation needed**: Haven't quantified personalization impact (social = 2.8x proven)

**❌ Trade-offs Made**:
- **Miss early personalization adopters**: Some customers want AI "now"
  - *Rationale*: Majority care more about engagement than personalization (validated in interviews)

## Dependencies & Risks

### Key Dependencies

**Technical**:
- **Wearable APIs**: Apple HealthKit, Fitbit Web API (access granted, documentation good)
- **HRIS Integrations**: Workday, BambooHR APIs (partnerships in progress)
- **Mobile App Stores**: Apple App Store, Google Play Store (2-week approval process)
- **Data Infrastructure**: Scalable backend for real-time leaderboards (AWS/GCP)

**Go-to-Market**:
- **Pilot Customers**: Need 20 willing customers for Q4 pilots (pipeline exists)
- **Sales Team**: Hire 2 AEs in Q1 to scale beyond founders selling
- **Marketing Content**: Case studies, testimonials, ROI calculator (create in Q1)

**Organizational**:
- **Eng Team**: Need 3 mobile engineers (iOS, Android, full-stack) - hiring in progress
- **Design Resources**: 1 product designer (contractor secured for Q4)
- **Customer Success**: 1 CSM by Q1 to support 20 pilots (hiring)

### Risks & Mitigations

**Risk 1: MVP Engagement Lower Than Expected (69% → 40%)**
- **Mitigation**: Monitor weekly engagement during pilots, iterate rapidly on social features
- **Contingency**: If below 50% by week 4, pause pilots and fix core engagement loop

**Risk 2: Pilot → Customer Conversion Fails (< 50%)**
- **Mitigation**: Strong pilot onboarding, weekly check-ins, gather feedback early
- **Contingency**: Extend pilots to 6 months (vs. 3), reduce price for early customers

**Risk 3: Wellable Responds Competitively (Improves Team Features)**
- **Mitigation**: Move fast (Q4 launch), lock in customers before Wellable reacts
- **Contingency**: Emphasize mobile UX + social-first design (harder to copy)

**Risk 4: Wearable Integration Issues Delay Launch**
- **Mitigation**: Manual entry fallback, start with 2 wearables (Apple + Fitbit)
- **Contingency**: Launch with manual entry only, add wearables in V1.1 (Jan 2026)
