## Scoring Dimensions

### 1. Strategic Alignment (Weight: 25%)
*How well does this opportunity align with our strategic themes and vision?*

**Scoring Criteria (1-5 scale):**
- **5 - Critical Alignment**: Directly advances multiple strategic themes, essential for vision
- **4 - Strong Alignment**: Clearly supports 1-2 strategic themes
- **3 - Moderate Alignment**: Some connection to strategic themes
- **2 - Weak Alignment**: Minimal strategic value
- **1 - No Alignment**: Doesn't support current strategy

**Strategic Theme Mapping:**
- **Theme 1**: [Does this strongly/moderately/weakly support Theme 1?]
- **Theme 2**: [Does this strongly/moderately/weakly support Theme 2?]
- **Theme 3**: [Does this strongly/moderately/weakly support Theme 3?]

### 2. Customer Impact (Weight: 30%)
*How significantly will this improve customer experience and outcomes?*

**Scoring Criteria (1-5 scale):**
- **5 - Transformational**: Fundamentally improves customer success, major workflow enhancement
- **4 - High Impact**: Significant improvement to customer experience
- **3 - Moderate Impact**: Noticeable improvement for customers
- **2 - Minor Impact**: Small improvement or niche benefit
- **1 - Minimal Impact**: Negligible customer benefit

**Impact Assessment:**
- **Breadth**: [How many customers affected?]
- **Depth**: [How significantly are they affected?]
- **Frequency**: [How often do customers encounter this need?]
- **Criticality**: [How essential is this to customer success?]

### 3. Business Value (Weight: 25%)
*What business outcomes will this drive?*

**Scoring Criteria (1-5 scale):**
- **5 - High Business Value**: Major revenue/retention impact, significant competitive advantage
- **4 - Strong Business Value**: Clear business benefits, measurable ROI
- **3 - Moderate Business Value**: Some business benefits
- **2 - Limited Business Value**: Minimal business impact
- **1 - No Business Value**: No clear business benefit

**Business Metrics:**
- **Revenue Impact**: [New revenue, expansion revenue potential]
- **Retention Impact**: [Effect on churn reduction]
- **Efficiency Impact**: [Cost savings, operational improvements]
- **Competitive Impact**: [Market position improvement]

### 4. Implementation Effort (Weight: 10%)
*How much effort is required to deliver this?*

**Scoring Criteria (1-5 scale, inverse scoring - lower effort = higher score):**
- **5 - Very Low Effort**: < 1 person-week, no dependencies
- **4 - Low Effort**: 1-4 person-weeks, minimal dependencies  
- **3 - Moderate Effort**: 1-3 person-months, some dependencies
- **2 - High Effort**: 3-6 person-months, multiple dependencies
- **1 - Very High Effort**: > 6 person-months, complex dependencies

**Effort Factors:**
- **Development Effort**: [Engineering time required]
- **Design Effort**: [Design and research time]
- **Technical Complexity**: [Architecture changes, integrations]
- **Dependencies**: [External dependencies and blockers]

### 5. Risk Assessment (Weight: 10%)
*What are the risks associated with this opportunity?*

**Scoring Criteria (1-5 scale, lower risk = higher score):**
- **5 - Very Low Risk**: Well-understood problem, proven solutions
- **4 - Low Risk**: Minor uncertainty, manageable risks
- **3 - Moderate Risk**: Some uncertainty, medium complexity
- **2 - High Risk**: Significant unknowns, high complexity
- **1 - Very High Risk**: Major unknowns, unproven concepts

**Risk Factors:**
- **Technical Risk**: [Technology or implementation risks]
- **Market Risk**: [Market acceptance or timing risks]
- **Resource Risk**: [Team capacity or skill risks]
- **Execution Risk**: [Delivery or coordination risks]

### Priority Tiers

**P0 - Must Have (Score: 4.0-5.0)**
- Critical for strategic success
- High customer and business impact
- Immediate implementation recommended

**P1 - Should Have (Score: 3.0-3.9)**  
- Important for strategic goals
- Significant value with manageable effort
- Schedule within next 2 quarters

**P2 - Could Have (Score: 2.0-2.9)**
- Valuable but not critical
- Good return on investment
- Consider for future roadmap

**P3 - Won't Have (Score: 1.0-1.9)**
- Low strategic or customer value
- High effort or risk
- Deprioritize for now
