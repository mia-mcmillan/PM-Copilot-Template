You are conducting a comprehensive SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) analysis for strategic planning.

## Your Task

Generate a detailed SWOT analysis based on current product context:

1. **Gather Context**: Review:
   - Recent customer insights and feedback
   - Competitive intelligence and market research
   - Current product capabilities and roadmap
   - Pattern analyses and strategic synthesis documents
   - Technical debt and capability gaps

2. **Analyze Each Dimension**:

   **STRENGTHS** (Internal, Positive):
   - Unique product capabilities
   - Strong customer relationships or segments
   - Technical advantages or IP
   - Team expertise or organizational capabilities
   - Market position or brand strength

   **WEAKNESSES** (Internal, Negative):
   - Product gaps or limitations
   - Technical debt or infrastructure issues
   - Resource constraints
   - Process inefficiencies
   - Market perception issues

   **OPPORTUNITIES** (External, Positive):
   - Market trends favorable to product
   - Unserved customer needs
   - Competitive vulnerabilities to exploit
   - Technology or ecosystem developments
   - Partnership or expansion possibilities

   **THREATS** (External, Negative):
   - Competitive moves or new entrants
   - Market shifts or declining segments
   - Technology disruptions
   - Regulatory or compliance risks
   - Economic or industry headwinds

3. **Create SWOT Document**: Generate file in `/analyses/` with:
   ```markdown
   # [Period] - SWOT Analysis

   **Date**: [Date]
   **Context**: [Product/market context for this analysis]
   **Sources**: [Data sources used]

   ## Executive Summary
   [2-3 paragraph overview of key findings]

   ## Strengths

   ### S1: [Strength Name]
   **Impact**: [High/Medium/Low]
   **Evidence**: [Supporting data or references]
   **Strategic Value**: [How this creates competitive advantage]
   **Sustainability**: [How long this advantage will last]

   [Repeat for each strength]

   ## Weaknesses

   ### W1: [Weakness Name]
   **Impact**: [High/Medium/Low]
   **Evidence**: [Supporting data or references]
   **Risk**: [What this weakness exposes us to]
   **Addressability**: [How difficult/costly to fix]

   [Repeat for each weakness]

   ## Opportunities

   ### O1: [Opportunity Name]
   **Impact**: [High/Medium/Low]
   **Evidence**: [Market data or customer insights]
   **Time Sensitivity**: [How urgent to pursue]
   **Feasibility**: [Our ability to capture this opportunity]
   **Alignment**: [How this fits strategic themes]

   [Repeat for each opportunity]

   ## Threats

   ### T1: [Threat Name]
   **Impact**: [High/Medium/Low]
   **Evidence**: [Market intelligence or competitive data]
   **Likelihood**: [Probability of occurrence]
   **Time Horizon**: [When this threat materializes]
   **Mitigation**: [How to defend against this threat]

   [Repeat for each threat]

   ## Strategic Implications

   ### SO Strategies (Strength-Opportunity)
   [How to use strengths to pursue opportunities]

   ### ST Strategies (Strength-Threat)
   [How to use strengths to mitigate threats]

   ### WO Strategies (Weakness-Opportunity)
   [How to overcome weaknesses to pursue opportunities]

   ### WT Strategies (Weakness-Threat)
   [How to minimize weaknesses and avoid threats]

   ## Priority Actions
   1. [Most critical actions based on SWOT]

   ## Review Schedule
   [When to update this analysis]
   ```

4. **Connect to Strategy**: Link SWOT findings to:
   - Current strategic themes
   - Roadmap priorities
   - Resource allocation decisions

## Output

Present:
- Key SWOT findings (top items in each category)
- Most critical strategic implications
- File path to SWOT analysis document
- Recommended priority actions
