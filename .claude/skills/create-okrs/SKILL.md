You are developing Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) aligned with strategic themes and product vision.

## Your Task

Create measurable OKRs that drive strategic outcomes:

1. **Gather Strategic Context**:
   - Current strategic themes from `/strategy/`
   - Product vision and long-term goals
   - Roadmap priorities and initiatives
   - Previous OKR results and learnings
   - Key metrics and baseline performance

2. **OKR Best Practices**:
   - **Objectives**: Qualitative, inspirational, time-bound goals
   - **Key Results**: Quantitative, measurable, ambitious outcomes
   - 3-5 Objectives per period
   - 3-5 Key Results per Objective
   - Mix of input and output metrics
   - Ambitious but achievable (70% success rate expected)

3. **OKR Structure Guidelines**:

   **Objective Criteria**:
   - Aligns with strategic theme
   - Inspirational and memorable
   - Time-bound (quarter or year)
   - Clearly states desired outcome

   **Key Result Criteria**:
   - Measurable with number/percentage
   - Has clear baseline and target
   - Ambitious (stretch goal)
   - Outcome-focused (not activity)
   - Verifiable (can prove success)

4. **Create OKR Document**: Generate in `/strategy/` with:
   ```markdown
   # [Period] - OKRs

   **Period**: [Q1 2025 or FY 2025]
   **Team/Product**: [Scope]
   **Owner**: [PM Name]
   **Status**: [Draft/Committed/In Progress/Completed]
   **Last Updated**: [Date]

   [1-2 paragraphs on why these OKRs and how they support strategic themes]

   ### Strategic Theme Alignment
   This set of OKRs advances:
   - [[Strategic Theme 1]] - [How it supports]
   - [[Strategic Theme 2]] - [How it supports]

   ## OKR Summary

   | Objective | Status | Progress | Confidence |
   |-----------|--------|----------|------------|
   | [O1 Name] | 🟢/🟡/🔴 | [X%] | [H/M/L] |
   | [O2 Name] | 🟢/🟡/🔴 | [X%] | [H/M/L] |

   ---

   ## Objective 1: [Objective Statement]

   **Strategic Theme**: [[Theme Name]]
   **Type**: [Growth/Retention/Efficiency/Quality]
   **Owner**: [Responsible PM]
   **Status**: 🟢 On Track | 🟡 At Risk | 🔴 Behind
   **Overall Progress**: [X%] complete
   **Confidence**: [High/Medium/Low]

   ### Why This Objective
   [1-2 paragraphs explaining the strategic importance and expected impact]

   ### Key Results

   **Type**: [Growth/Retention/Engagement/Quality]
   **Metric**: [Specific metric name]

   **Progress**:
   | Date | Value | % to Target | Notes |
   |------|-------|-------------|-------|
   | [Date] | [Value] | [X%] | [Context] |

   **Supporting Initiatives**:
   - [[Roadmap Initiative 1]] - [How it contributes]
   - [[Roadmap Initiative 2]] - [How it contributes]

   **Risks**:
   - [Risk to achieving this KR]

   **Mitigation**:
   - [How to address risks]

   #### KR 1.2: [Another Key Result]
   [Same structure as KR 1.1]

   #### KR 1.3: [Third Key Result]
   [Same structure as KR 1.1]

   ---

   ## Objective 2: [Second Objective]
   [Full structure as Objective 1]

   ---

   ## Objective 3: [Third Objective]
   [Full structure as Objective 1]

   ---

   ## Cross-Objective Dependencies
   [Describe how objectives relate or depend on each other]

   |-----------|---------------|----------|
   | Objective 1 | 40% | P0 |
   | Objective 2 | 35% | P0 |
   | Objective 3 | 25% | P1 |

   ## Risks to OKR Achievement

   ### High Risk
   - **[Risk]**: [Description]
     - **Impact**: [What happens if this risk materializes]
     - **Mitigation**: [How to address]
     - **Owner**: [Who owns mitigation]

   ### Medium Risk
   [Same structure]

   ## Success Scenario
   [Describe what success looks like if we achieve these OKRs]

   ## Failure Scenario
   [Describe what happens if we don't achieve these OKRs]

   ## Review Cadence

   **Monthly**: [Deep dive on progress and course correction]
   **End of Period**: [Retrospective and grading]

   ## Stakeholder Communication
   **Executives**: [What and when to communicate]
   **Team**: [How to keep team aligned]
   **Customers**: [Any customer-facing communication]

   ## Grading Criteria
   - **1.0 (100%)**: Exceptional, exceeded expectations
   - **0.7 (70%)**: Success, achieved ambitious goal
   - **0.5 (50%)**: Partial success, made progress
   - **0.3 (30%)**: Struggled, minimal progress
   - **0.0 (0%)**: Failed, no meaningful progress

   ## Historical Context
   **Previous Period OKRs**: [[Link to previous OKRs]]
   **Learnings Applied**: [How past results informed these OKRs]

   ## Appendix

   ### Metric Definitions
   **[Metric Name]**: [Clear definition of how this is calculated]

   ### Supporting Data
   [Links to dashboards, analyses, or data sources]
   ```

5. **Validate OKRs**: Check that:
   - Each Objective aligns with strategic theme
   - Key Results are measurable and ambitious
   - Mix of leading and lagging indicators
   - Baselines and targets are realistic but stretching
   - Clear ownership and tracking plan

6. **Create Supporting Artifacts**:
   - OKR dashboard template
   - Weekly tracking format
   - Monthly review template
   - End-of-period grading template

## Output

Present:
- Summary of OKRs created (Objectives and KR count)
- Strategic alignment validation
- Resource allocation breakdown
- File path to OKR document
- Recommended tracking and review cadence
